## Project Overview

A 90-acre former Boeing campus was transformed into a next-generation aerospace and innovation hub.

## Our Role

John Keisler led economic development efforts to attract key industry tenants.

## Challenge

The site required rezoning, investment attraction, and a strategy to support emerging aerospace industries.

## Approach

Economic development initiatives, federal grant funding, and private-sector partnerships were leveraged to attract anchor tenants and support industry growth.

## Results

- 90-acre innovation district established
- Federal grants secured for workforce and supply chain development
- Aerospace and space technology companies attracted to the site
