## Project Overview

The City of Long Beach sought to modernize waterfront assets and strengthen tourism-driven economic activity.

## Our Role

John Keisler led efforts to restructure agreements and support private investment attraction.

## Challenge

Aging facilities lacked sufficient revenue to support capital improvements and long-term reinvestment.

## Approach

Public-private partnerships and revenue-sharing agreements were restructured to improve asset performance and attract investment.

## Results

- Historic Queen Mary reopened
- Convention Center revenues strengthened
- Breakers Hotel renovation supported
- $1.6B+ annual economic impact district enhanced
