## Project Overview

Revitalized the Historic Queen Mary through strategic partnerships and private investment.

## Our Role

As Economic Development Director for the City of Long Beach, John Keisler led efforts to restructure public-private partnerships and attract private investment for the Queen Mary's revitalization.

## Challenge

The aging landmark required capital investment and a sustainable operating model.

## Approach

Public-private partnerships were restructured, operating agreements were renegotiated, and revenue-sharing strategies were implemented to support long-term revitalization.

## Results

- Historic Queen Mary reopened to the public
- Positive net operating income achieved
- Increased private investment in the waterfront district
- Strengthened Long Beach's tourism and entertainment district
