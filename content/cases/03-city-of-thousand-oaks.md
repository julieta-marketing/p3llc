## Project Overview

Developed a public-private partnership strategy for a mixed-use downtown redevelopment.

## Our Role

Fullerton Consulting Partners provided financial and P3 advisory services in collaboration with HR&A.

## Challenge

The City needed a financing and development strategy to advance its long-term vision.

## Approach

Prepared financial analysis, funding strategies, P3 structure, and developer procurement documents.

## Results

- Financial feasibility completed
- Funding strategy developed
- P3 structure established
- Procurement documents prepared
