## Project Overview

Replaced an aging courthouse with a modern justice facility delivered through a DBFOM public-private partnership.

## Our Role

Jeff Fullerton provided development services for the lead contractor, including lease negotiations, off-site improvements, community relations, and investor relations.

## Challenge

An outdated courthouse was impacting court operations and service delivery.

## Approach

Implemented a DBFOM delivery model with an availability agreement to ensure uninterrupted operations.

## Results

- Delivered on time and on budget
- 31 courtrooms and detention facility
- No disruption to court operations
