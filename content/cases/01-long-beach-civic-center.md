## Project Overview

The City of Long Beach sought to replace aging civic facilities with a modern, integrated civic campus.

## Our Role

Jeff Fullerton served as Project Director, leading project planning, community outreach, and stakeholder engagement that resulted in unanimous project approval.

## Challenge

Aging infrastructure required significant investment, coordination, and long-term risk management.

## Approach

A DBFOM public-private partnership aligned public goals with private-sector financing, development, and operations.

## Results

- $1B+ civic redevelopment delivered
- New City Hall, Main Library, and Port Headquarters
- 743 housing units
- 4-acre public park
